# Team and access

Invite your team, give each person the right role, and sign in with your identity provider.

## Invite members

Add several email addresses at once and choose a role. You are billed only for people who commit, not for invitations.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including billing and deleting the organisation. There is always exactly one. |
| Admin | Configure policies, integrations and members. |
| Member | Work on findings: triage, assign, ignore with a reason. |
| Viewer | Read everything, change nothing. |

## SSO and SCIM

On Enterprise, members sign in through Okta, Entra ID or Google Workspace, and SCIM keeps the member list in sync with your directory.
