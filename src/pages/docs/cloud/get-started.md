# Get started with Cloud

Connect your source control and get your first findings in a few minutes. Free up to 10 contributors, no credit card.

## Set it up

1. [Create an account](https://app.oauthlint.dev/signup) with GitHub, or with an email and password.
2. Name your organisation and pick its URL.
3. Connect your source control: GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket or Azure DevOps, cloud or self-managed.
4. Choose the repositories to scan. You can add more later.
5. The first scan starts right away. Most teams see their first finding within two minutes.
