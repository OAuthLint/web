Data Processing Agreement

Last updated 2026-09-01 · GDPR Article 28

This agreement applies when you use OAuthLint Cloud and it processes personal data on your behalf. You are the controller; Auspeo SAS is the processor.

What we process

Names and email addresses of your members, commit author names on findings, and any personal data inside code snippets you choose to send.

How we protect it

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256).
  • Access limited to on-call engineers, logged and reviewed.
  • Annual penetration test; summary available on request.

Sub-processors

The list on the Privacy Policy is part of this agreement. You can object to a new one within 30 days.

Breaches

We notify you without undue delay, and within 48 hours, of any breach affecting your data.

Signing

Team and Enterprise customers can sign a countersigned copy from Settings, Billing. Or write to legal@auspeo.dev.
