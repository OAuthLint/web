Frequently asked questions

Is it really open source?

Yes. The CLI and every rule are MIT. The Cloud app is source-available and free to self-host with the full scanning loop. Only the enterprise features need a license key.

Why pay for Cloud?

So you do not run the source-control integration, the workers, the database and the upgrades yourself. It is free for up to 10 contributors.

What counts as a contributor?

Anyone who authored a commit in a scanned repository in the last 30 days. Viewers and bots are free.

Do you clone our repositories?

No. We read the diff of a pull request and the files it touches, then discard the source when the scan ends.
